CSE 410 / 510 - Introduction to Agentic Engineering
University at Buffalo, Fall 2026

Instructors: Dr. Joaquin Carbonara and Dr. David Doermann

Lab 1: A Minimal Tool-Using Agent.
Assigned in Week 2, due in Week 4.

In this lab students build a small tool-using agent from scratch in Python.
The agent receives a natural-language question, decides which tool to use,
calls that tool, and returns an answer grounded in the tool's output.
